# KNYC Nowcaster — production review and improvement plan

Review date: 2026-11-16. Data: `server_pull/` (gitignored local copy of the server logs) (2026-09-24 → 2026-11-16, 54 NY-local days, 53 settled).
Code: commit `7c0e234`. No code or data was modified for this review.

Ground truth used here was re-fetched from IEM (CLINYC, DSMNYC, hourly ASOS for KNYC and the 8 upstream
stations). "Replay" means re-running the shipped `.pkl` models on the logged feature rows with specific
features corrected. "Training OOS" means `knyc_training.csv` rows after `train_through` (2022-01 → 2026-07,
~39.5k hours), scored with the shipped daily-high model.

---

## 1. Executive summary

- **Three train/serve feature bugs are live in production.** (a) Solar geometry is computed for a fixed date
  of ~21 Jan 1970 (datetime unit bug), (b) clear sky is never seen by the model (61% of live rows carry the
  fill value 4 oktas; truth is CLR), (c) KPHL/KBWI/KBOS inputs are one hour stale in ~98% of hours. Replaying
  the models with all three corrected: **t+3h MAE 1.44 → 1.27 °F** (bias −0.40 → +0.06), **t+6h 1.98 → 1.85**,
  daily high 1.70 → 1.65 (bias −0.17 → −0.01). The solar error is smallest right now (Nov–Jan) and grows to
  ~40° of solar altitude by June, so it gets much worse if left.
- **The evening "Reassessed High" is wrong by construction.** After 19:00 local, the rounded observed high
  equals the official CLI high in 92% of live hours (training OOS: 91%), but `reassessed = max(obs, pred)`
  matches only 49–64% because the model keeps predicting ~0.5 °F above a high that is already in.
- **The prediction tape picks its bracket from `pred_raw`, ignoring the observed floor.** Tape bracket hit
  rate at 19–23h is 9%; overall 33% vs 72% for the market favourite.
- **The model has no edge against the Kalshi market at any hour.** Market-implied point forecast MAE is
  ~0.85 °F overnight vs the model's 2.2–3.1 °F. Ladder Brier: model 0.698 vs market 0.273 (uniform = 0.833;
  the model is worse than uniform before 06:00). When the model's bracket disagrees with the favourite
  (539 hours), the model is right 14% and the favourite 70%. Best blend weight on the model is 0.00 in five
  of six hour blocks.
- **The scorer's strategies lose money on this data.** `run_flat` (best "edge" contract): 1,214 bets, 9% hit
  rate, ROI −43% (day-clustered 95% CI −76% to −1%). `run_rebalanced`: bankroll 1,000 → 0.00. The "edge" is
  adverse selection: the residual model is miscalibrated (it says ≥80% and is right 61%).
- **Seasonal drift is real but not fixable by a bias correction.** Daily-high bias: Sep +0.93, Oct −0.04,
  Nov −0.95 °F. It survives the feature fixes, the same model shows no such monthly bias over 4.5 OOS years,
  and a trailing-N-day bias correction made OOS MAE worse in every configuration tested.
- **Data integrity and operations are in good shape.** 1 missed hour in 1,285 (KNYC METAR itself was absent),
  no ragged rows, DST fall-back handled correctly, `actual_high` equals the CLI max on all 53 settled days,
  and the end-of-day market leader matched the CLI bracket on all 53. Errors are transient 5xx and Discord
  503 handshakes that self-recovered.
- **Sample size caveat.** 53 settled days, with hours inside a day strongly correlated. Accuracy and Brier
  conclusions are solid; every ROI number is noisy and all CIs here are day-clustered bootstraps.

## 2. Findings

### F1. Solar features are computed for January 1970 (unit bug) — confirmed exactly

`knyc_discord_bot.py:382` (`solar_zenith_deg`): `jd = t.astype("int64") / 86_400_000_000_000 + 2440587.5`
assumes nanosecond resolution. On the server the `valid` column is `datetime64[us]`, so the Julian day is
~1970-01-21 for every row; only the hour angle is right.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Logged `solar_zenith` vs formula with microsecond input | max abs diff **0.0000°** (exact match, 1,284 rows) |
| Logged `solar_zenith` vs correct value | mean abs diff 8.1°, max 19.9° |
| Mean noon `solar_alt`, live vs training rows for the same calendar window | 29.0° vs 38.2° |

It does not reproduce locally (local pandas 2.3.3 yields `ns`): the Sept local log is 144/144 correct, while
36 of 91 July rows show the same bug. The identical line exists at `build_training_data.py:241`; the
training CSV on disk was built correctly, but a rebuild under a different pandas would poison it.

Replay impact (solar only): t+3h MAE 1.438 → 1.32, t+6h 1.976 → 1.93, daily high 1.70 → 1.69.

### F2. Clear sky is never reported to the model

aviationweather.gov returns `"clouds": []` with a top-level `"cover": "CLR"` for clear sky (verified live
today for KNYC). `fetch_avwx` reads only `clouds[0]`, so `skyc1..3` are NaN, `cloud_oktas` is NaN, then
`ffill(limit=3).fillna(4)`.

| `cloud_oktas` | 0 | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Live log (1,284 h) | 0 | 90 | 784 | 82 | 328 |
| IEM truth for the same hours | 827 | 43 | 42 | 62 | 310 |
| Training, same calendar window | 57.7% | 4.2% | 3.3% | 7.0% | 27.8% |

Live matches IEM in 35.6% of hours; 742 hours were truly clear but logged as 4. `ceiling_ft` is unaffected
(100% agreement). Replay impact (cloud only): daily high MAE 1.70 → 1.67, overnight bias −0.54 → −0.24.

### F5. Climate day is local STANDARD time; the bot uses the NY civil day

CLINYC and DSMNYC report on a midnight-to-midnight LST day all year. During EDT that is 01:00–01:00 EDT.
Evidence: CLI and DSM high-times agree to the minute on 44 of 53 days (DSM is LST by definition), and on
2026-10-17 the CLI max is 62 at "12:08 AM" while the 00:51 EDT ob was 63 °F — only consistent if 00:51 EDT
belongs to Oct 16.

- `get_nowcast` builds the floor from `df["local_date"] == today_local` (NY civil date). On Oct 17 the floor
  was 62.96 for all 24 hours while the settlement was 62: every hour had a floor above the truth.
- Training OOS: the civil-day floor exceeds the CLI target on 19 of 1,660 days (16 of them in DST); an
  LST-day floor on 9.
- `score_predictions.fetch_cli` / `fetch_dsm` stamp the high-time with `tzinfo=NY_TZ`, so during DST
  `high_time` is one hour early and `score_daily_high`'s "issued before the high" filter is off by an hour.

Dormant until DST resumes on 2027-03-14, but cheap to fix now.

### F9. Operations

- **Errors are benign.** 23 aviationweather 5xx warnings (all recovered by retry), 4 Discord gateway 503
  handshake tracebacks on Nov 13 13:42 (auto-reconnected), 1 Kalshi 503, 1 hour with the IEM fallback also
  failing. No tracebacks from bot code, no OOM (peak 190 MB).
- **Restarts cluster just around the 06:30 ET scorecard.** Sep 26 06:49, Oct 1 06:40, Oct 21 06:32,
  Oct 23 06:28, Nov 13 06:29 (server clock is ET). That pattern fits Ubuntu's `apt-daily-upgrade.timer`
  (06:00 + up to 60 min) restarting services, though the excerpted log cannot confirm the trigger. A restart
  that straddles 06:30:00 would silently skip that day's scorecard and chart; the log excerpt cannot show
  whether that happened.
- **Each restart re-posts and re-logs the current hour** via `on_ready`.
- **`deploy.sh` points at `.venv/`, the server runs from `venv/`** (traceback paths show
  `/home/john/NYC-Temp-Engine/venv/lib/python3.12`). Under `set -e` the script would stop at the pip step and
  never restart the service.
- `_get` in `score_predictions.py` retries only on 429; a single 503 from Kalshi or IEM raises immediately.
- 78 pandas `PerformanceWarning: DataFrame is highly fragmented` lines per excerpt, plus an XGBoost
  pickle-version warning on every start. Noise only.
- Kalshi's `rules_primary` text currently reads "...recorded at New York City (CLINYC) ... according to The
  Weather Company". Settlement has matched CLINYC on all 53 days, but the wording differs from what

## 3. Prioritized change list

Scope is sized for one implementation session: P0 and P1 are the session; P2 only if time remains.

### P0-1. Fix the solar Julian-day unit bug

- **Why:** F1. Exact-match evidence; biggest single contributor to the t+3h error.
- **Where:** `knyc_discord_bot.py` `solar_zenith_deg` (line 382); same line in `build_training_data.py:241`.
- **How:** replace the int64 cast with a unit-independent expression in both files:
  `jd = (t - pd.Timestamp("1970-01-01", tz="UTC")) / pd.Timedelta(days=1) + 2440587.5`.
  These are the only two `astype("int64")` uses in the repo.
- **Validate:**
  1. Unit test with the same timestamps cast to `datetime64[ns, UTC]`, `[us, UTC]` and `[s, UTC]`: results
     identical to 1e-9. This must be tested by casting — it does not reproduce on local pandas 2.3.3.
  2. Sanity value: KNYC 2026-06-21 17:00Z gives solar altitude 72.7°; 2026-11-16 17:00Z gives 30.2°.
  3. After deploy, first logged row: recompute zenith offline from `valid_t`; diff < 0.01°.

### P0-2. Parse clear sky from aviationweather

- **Why:** F2.
- **Where:** `knyc_discord_bot.py` `fetch_avwx`, the `rows.append({...})` block.
- **How:** when `clouds` is empty, fall back to the top-level field:
  `skyc1 = clouds[0]["cover"] if clouds else r.get("cover")` (leave `skyc2/3` and `skyl1` NaN). `SKY_TO_OKTAS`
  already maps CLR/SKC/NSC/NCD to 0. Also strip/upper before lookup (IEM returns `"VV "` with a trailing
  space; the existing `.strip().upper()` in `cloud_oktas` covers it).
- **Validate:** fetch KNYC for the last 72 h through `fetch_avwx` and through `fetch_iem`; `cloud_oktas`
  should agree on >90% of hours (today 35.6%) and the share of zeros should be near the IEM share.

### P0-3. Wait for late upstream stations; log once per posted hour

- **Why:** F3 (98% stale inputs for three stations), plus the duplicate rows and re-poll price skew in F8.
- **Where:** `knyc_discord_bot.py` `get_nowcast`, `post_nowcast_to_channel`, `on_ready`.
- **How:**
  1. Split `get_nowcast` into a side-effect-free `compute_nowcast()` (everything up to and including the
     prediction and floor logic) and `log_nowcast(data)` (the nowcast-log row, the Kalshi snapshot, the tape
     row). `compute_nowcast` returns the feature row and `today_local` so `log_nowcast` needs no refetch.
  2. In `compute_nowcast`, add `stale_upstream = [s for s in UPSTREAM if upstream[s].empty or
     upstream[s]["valid"].max() < valid_t]` to the returned dict (`fetch_avwx` already floors :54 obs onto
     the :51 grid, so the comparison is exact). Log it at INFO.
  3. In `post_nowcast_to_channel(wait_for_fresh=True)`: record the tick start; if the KNYC ob is fresh but
     `stale_upstream` is non-empty and less than `UPSTREAM_SYNC_WAIT = 8 min` has elapsed, sleep 60 s and
     recompute instead of posting. After the wait, post with whatever is there (the existing
     `MAX_DEGRADED_UPSTREAM` guard still applies to truly missing stations).
  4. Call `log_nowcast(data)` only on the attempt that is actually posted.
  5. At startup, initialise `last_posted_valid` from the last row of `nowcast_log.csv`; on the `on_ready`
     path still post the embed, but skip `log_nowcast` when `valid_t` is not newer than the last logged one.
  6. Keep `_append_log_row` exactly as is (header-aligned, null-filled, append-only).
- **Validate:** after 48 h in production, repeat the F3 comparison: KPHL/KBWI/KBOS should match the
  current-hour IEM value in >95% of detectable hours; `nowcast_log.csv` and `market_log.csv` gain zero new
  duplicate `valid_t`; posts land by about :03 past the hour.

### P0-4. Make the evening reassessed high follow the observations; use it for the tape bracket

- **Why:** F4.
- **Where:** `knyc_discord_bot.py` `get_nowcast` (the `reassessed = max(...)` line and the
  `tape.append_row(...)` call); `prediction_tape.append_row`.
- **How:**
  1. After computing `obs_high` and `pred_high`: if `obs_high is not None` and NY-local hour of `valid_t`
     is ≥ 19 and `cur_temp <= obs_high - 1.0`, set `reassessed = obs_high`; otherwise keep
     `max(obs_high, pred_high)`. Put the two thresholds in named constants.
  2. Change `append_row` to select the bracket with `km.bracket_for(brackets, reassessed)`. Keep the
     `pred_raw` and `pred_high` columns as the raw model output so the schema is unchanged.
  3. The embed's `obs_note` logic is unaffected.
- **Validate:** replay over `server_pull/nowcast_log.csv`: exact-match rate of `round(reassessed)` vs CLI at
  19–23h should be ≥0.90 (now 0.49–0.64) and MAE ≤0.2 (now 0.55–0.83); hours 0–18 unchanged.
- **Note:** this changes what the tape measures from the cut-over hour onward — see Decisions D3.

### P1-1. Use the LST climate day for the floor and for CLI/DSM times

- **Why:** F5. Dormant until 2027-03-14; guaranteed-wrong floor when it bites.
- **Where:** `knyc_discord_bot.py` `get_nowcast`; `score_predictions.py` `fetch_cli`, `fetch_dsm`.
- **How:**
  1. Add `CLIMATE_TZ = timezone(timedelta(hours=-5))`. Keep `today_local` (NY civil date) for the Kalshi
     event lookup. Build the floor from `df["valid"].dt.tz_convert(CLIMATE_TZ).dt.date == today_local`, and
     apply the same conversion to the 6-hour-max window check (`valid − 6h` must also fall on that climate
     date). At 00:51 EDT this correctly yields no floor yet.

### P1-2. Replace the residual CDF with an observation-conditioned offset PMF

- **Why:** F7. The current distribution ignores how far the model sits above the observed high, so it
  spreads mass upward all evening (Brier 0.69 at 19–23h) and is badly overconfident elsewhere.
- **Where:** `build_residuals.py` (new builder), `market_scorer.py` (`ResidualCDF`, `bracket_probs`).
  Produces a new artifact `offset_pmf.csv`; leave `residual_model.csv` in place.
- **How:**
  1. In `build_residuals.py`, on OOS training rows: reconstruct the observed high so far per LST climate day
     (cumulative max of hourly `tmpf`, plus the METAR 6-hour max when its window lies inside the climate
     day; forward-fill the cumulative 6-hour max within the day). Compute `re = max(pred, obs_so_far)`,
     `k = target_high − floor(re + 0.5)` clipped to [−8, 8], `gap = pred − obs_so_far` bucketed at
     (−inf, 0], (0, 1], (1, 2], (2, 4], (4, inf). Drop rows where the NY civil date differs from the LST
     date.
  2. Emit one row per (`local_hour`, gap bucket) with ≥150 samples holding the empirical frequency of each
     `k`, plus a pooled row per `local_hour`.
  3. In `market_scorer.py`, `bracket_probs` becomes: look up the PMF for (hour, gap bucket) falling back to
     the hour's pooled row; for each `k` add its weight to the contract containing
     `max(floor(re + 0.5) + k, round(obs_high))`; add 0.002 to every contract and renormalise.
- **Validate:** on `server_pull` data, ladder Brier should drop from 0.698 to ~0.57 overall and from 0.69 to
  ~0.19 at 19–23h; the top calibration bucket (≥0.95) should be right ~92% (now 66%). Expected reference
  PMF from the prototype, hours 19–23, gap ≤ 0: P(k=0) 0.961, P(k=1) 0.030.
- **Expectation to set:** this makes the scorer honest; it does not create an edge (market 0.273).

## 4. Decisions for the user

- **D1. Betting posture.** On this data the model's picks should not be traded: no hour block shows an edge,
  and the scorer's "edge" bets lose 43–57%. Options: (a) treat the Kalshi side as a scoreboard only, which
  is what the evidence supports; (b) keep collecting and revisit after the P0 fixes plus at least another
  60–90 settled days. The only positive number found — buying the market favourite at 0.5–0.9, roughly
  +4% to +9% after fees — is not a model signal and its CI spans zero.
- **D2. Retraining.** The P0 fixes need no retrain (the training CSV was built correctly). Closing the gap
  to the market does: the model has no NWP guidance and the market is ~2.5× more accurate before noon.
  A retrain worth doing would add (a) a forecast-guidance feature (NBM/MOS/HRRR max-temp for the day) and
  (b) "observed high so far" and "hours into the climate day" as features. That is a multi-session project
  and needs a historical guidance archive; it is not in this plan's scope.
- **D3. Tape semantics.** P0-4 switches the tape bracket from the raw model value to the reassessed value.
  Hit rate and `ret_per_dollar` will not be comparable across the cut-over. Recommended, with the cut-over
  date noted in the README.
- **D4. Kalshi rules wording.** The contract text now mentions "The Weather Company" alongside CLINYC.
  Settlement matched CLINYC on 53/53 days, but it is worth reading the current rules once.
- **D5. Server restarts.** Confirm on the server what restarts the unit around 06:30 ET
  (`systemctl list-timers`, `journalctl -u knyc-bot | grep -c "Posted scorecard"`), and whether scorecards
  for Sep 26, Oct 1, Oct 21, Oct 23 and Nov 13 appeared in #score.
- **D6. pandas on the server.** P0-1 removes the unit dependence. Pinning `pandas<3` in
  `requirements.txt` is an optional extra guard; say if you want it.

## 5. Things not to change, and why

- **Do not add a rolling or seasonal bias correction**, despite the visible Sep +0.9 / Nov −0.9 bias. Tested
  on 4.5 OOS years in 12 configurations; every one increased MAE (2.658 → 2.665–2.830).
- **Do not rewrite or migrate the logs.** No schema drift occurred; `_append_log_row`'s header-aligned
  null-fill worked. New diagnostics go to the journal or a separate file, not new columns.
- **Do not change settlement truth.** CLI-only via `fetch_cli` (explicit `limit`, summary-date parsing)
  matched on 53/53 days and agreed with the market's end-of-day leader on 53/53.
- **Do not touch bracket interval semantics or `round_half_up`.** Ladders tiled with exactly one winner in
  all 1,270 ladder-hours.
- **Do not change `MAX_DEGRADED_UPSTREAM` or the IEM fallback.** The one degraded hour behaved as designed.
- **Do not bother fixing `feel_gap`.** It is always 0 live but non-zero in 24% of fall and 71–79% of winter
  training rows. Zeroing it on winter OOS rows changes MAE by ≤0.003 °F on all three models.
- **Do not add a midnight-interpolation floor** for post-midnight highs (Nov 13 type). Tested on training
  OOS: it closes the gap on those days from 2.25 to 2.04 °F and recovers only 12% of them. Needs sub-hourly
  observations to solve properly.
- **Do not tune Kelly/band/edge parameters** on 53 days. `--sweep` on a strategy with a negative expected
  edge only finds the least-bad noise.
- **Do not worry about whole-°F vs tenth-°C temperatures.** Training `tmpf` is whole °F, live is tenth-°C
  converted; the difference is <0.1 °F.
